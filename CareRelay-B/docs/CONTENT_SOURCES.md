The source registry records stable IDs, publisher, title, URL and checked_on. Every anatomy/state/follow-up card refers to those IDs; voice scripts reuse the same cards so captions do not drift from the content. No source endorses CareRelay or validates its detection rules.

The four views are product teaching groupings, not four formal clinical stages. The progressing view illustrates macular swelling without asserting a required chronological stage. The explicit disclaimer and progression limit are product safety text, not source quotations.

review_status is source_checked_prototype_clinical_review_pending. Source review and automated reference checks are complete; no qualified clinical review has occurred. Do not describe the material as clinically approved. No patient disease-stage assignment, treatment selection or timing recommendation is made.

Voice approval means inclusion in a fixed prototype allowlist. Unsupported questions receive a refusal; it is not a comprehensive triage assistant. Patient facts remain separate source-record statements and must not be used to infer retinal disease.
